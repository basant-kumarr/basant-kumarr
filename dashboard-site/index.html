Featured project · Machine learning · Team project

Fraud Detection

Machine Learning

An explainable model that catches the fraud a standard baseline misses, framed for a fraud operations team rather than a leaderboard.

  • 99.9%AUC-ROC
  • 97.2%Precision
  • 496Of 644 frauds caught, 437 more than the baseline
  • 555,719Synthetic transactions analysed

Public synthetic dataset from Kaggle, not real card-network data. Synthetic data is cleaner than real transactions, so the useful part is the explainable behavioural features, not the headline score.

Live Dashboard Repository

Python · XGBoost · GridSearchCV · SHAP · scikit-learn · Gradio

Problem
A logistic regression baseline caught only 59 of 644 fraudulent transactions in the test set. Can a model do better, and explain why it flags what it flags?
Approach
Behavioural features (spending deviation from the cardholder average, distance to merchant, card-not-present proxy), then tuned XGBoost with a precision-first threshold, explained with SHAP.
My role
MSc team coursework (MI6228, Maynooth University). My contribution: feature engineering, XGBoost tuning, SHAP analysis and Gradio deployment.

Featured project · Client consultancy · Team project

Age Friendly Ireland

Data Analysis & Visualisation

A real client engagement for Age Friendly Ireland: how ageing will change who struggles with stairs at home, and what national policy should do about it.

  • 349,155People with mobility difficulties (Census 2022)
  • 22.3%Households affected by 2050, up from 16.9% in 2022
  • 100,000+Additional households affected by 2050 without policy change
  • 5Policy proposals delivered to the client
View Repository CSO Census 2022 · CSO PEC19 projections · Prevalence modelling · Policy benchmarking
Problem
Age Friendly Ireland needed a defensible estimate of how many Irish households face stair-related mobility barriers today and in future decades.
Approach
Census 2022 prevalence by age and sex, applied to CSO PEC19 population projections and converted to household level, then benchmarked against Australia, Sweden and Denmark.
Context
Four-person MSc Business Analytics team. Five recommendations delivered to the client in an executive presentation.

Featured project · Forecasting

Sales Demand Forecasting

Business Analytics

Forecasting built around a planning decision: too much stock ties up capital, too little loses sales.

  • R
  • ARIMA
  • Prophet
  • ggplot2
  • Forecast demand
  • Plan inventory
  • Reduce stockouts
  • Avoid overstock
View Repository
Problem
Inaccurate demand forecasts lead to excess inventory or stockouts. Which model gives planners a forecast they can order against?
Approach
Trend decomposition and ADF stationarity tests, then Auto ARIMA and Prophet in R, evaluated with rolling-window validation on RMSE, MAE and MAPE.
Finding
Both improved on a naive baseline. Prophet did better on strongly seasonal series with holiday effects; ARIMA on stationary, trend-driven series.
